How to play.

01 · LAUNCH

Press launch or Space. Each round alternates 10 seconds of autonomous physics with 10 seconds of manual control.

02 · STEER

Drag the joystick or use WASD / arrow keys to influence movement. DASH adds forward thrust. TILT trades speed for a stronger, less stable impact. Choose only one special action per manual phase.

03 · AVATAR ATTACK

When Burst Health falls to 50% or less, manually activate your Core's unique Avatar once this round.

04 · WIN THE MATCH

Ring Out: 2 points · Spin Finish: 1 point · Burst Finish: 3 points. First to 5 wins. Build stats alter mass, grip, drag, spin reserve, and impact.
