03 / READ THE RING

Win the long game.

Rival moves stay hidden until both actions resolve. A charge or dodge can be punished by a well-timed attack. Repeating a pattern too long makes you readable.

Pressure pushes a Bey toward ring-out; energy drains every turn. The stadium destabilizes gradually, making late rounds riskier.

04 / THE ENGINE

Chance, not chaos.

Collisions use a mobility- and action-based probability. Impact gets a small ±10% variance. Burst odds are nonlinear and capped; a big special raises risk but never guarantees a finish.

Match ends by burst, ring-out, or spin-out. Simultaneous finishes are draws. At turn 40, judges compare remaining energy, stability, and pressure.
